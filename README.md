<h2>A little about me 🧭</h2>

<p>I'm a UX/UI designer with a frontend development background. At the Kazan Federal University digital team I worked on the redesign of the university web portal: I fixed navigation and user-flow problems, designed 80+ adaptive screens from wireframes to interactive prototypes, and worked within the project's design system.</p>

<p>Before that I spent over a year as a frontend developer at PetPortal, building the interface in React and TypeScript side by side with the product's UX/UI designer. That's where I fell in love with design, and it's why I now speak both languages: the designer's and the developer's.</p>

<details>
  <summary><b>✨ A few things I enjoy working on</b></summary>
  <br>
  <ul>
    <li>Turning messy scenarios into clear user flows and information architecture</li>
    <li>Going from wireframes to polished, adaptive mockups and prototypes in Figma</li>
    <li>Building and growing design systems and reusable components</li>
    <li>Testing ideas with real people: user interviews and usability testing</li>
    <li>Bringing my own designs to life through code</li>
  </ul>
