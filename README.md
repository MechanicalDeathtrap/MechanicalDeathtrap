A little about me 🧭
I'm a UX/UI designer with a frontend development background. At the Kazan Federal University digital team I worked on the redesign of the university web portal: I fixed navigation and user-flow problems, designed 80+ adaptive screens from wireframes to interactive prototypes, and worked within the project's design system.
Before that I spent over a year as a frontend developer at PetPortal, building the interface in React and TypeScript side by side with the product's UX/UI designer. That's where I fell in love with design, and it's why I now speak both languages: the designer's and the developer's.
